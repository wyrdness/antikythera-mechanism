# Sources & References

This document provides comprehensive academic and scientific sources for the Antikythera Mechanism. All major claims in the documentation are supported by peer-reviewed research and primary archaeological evidence.

## Primary Scientific Publications

### Foundational Research

1. **Price, D.J. de Solla** (1974). "Gears from the Greeks: The Antikythera Mechanism—A Calendar Computer from ca. 80 B.C." *Transactions of the American Philosophical Society*, 64(7), 1-70. DOI: 10.2307/1006146
   - Pioneering scientific analysis establishing the mechanism as an astronomical computer

2. **Freeth, T., Bitsakis, Y., Moussas, X., et al.** (2006). "Decoding the ancient Greek astronomical calculator known as the Antikythera Mechanism." *Nature*, 444(7119), 587-591. DOI: 10.1038/nature05357
   - Breakthrough CT scanning study revealing internal structure and functions

3. **Freeth, T., Jones, A., Steele, J.M., & Bitsakis, Y.** (2008). "Calendars with Olympiad display and eclipse prediction on the Antikythera Mechanism." *Nature*, 454(7204), 614-617. DOI: 10.1038/nature07130
   - Decoded rear dials including Saros cycle eclipse predictor and Games calendar

### Recent Major Studies

4. **Jones, A.** (2017). "The Inscriptions of the Antikythera Mechanism." *Almagest*, 8(1), 102-158. DOI: 10.1484/J.ALMAGEST.5.113944
   - Comprehensive decipherment of over 3,000 characters of Greek text

5. **Freeth, T.** (2014). "Eclipse Prediction on the Ancient Greek Astronomical Calculating Machine Known as the Antikythera Mechanism." *PLoS ONE*, 9(7), e103275. DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0103275
   - Detailed analysis of eclipse prediction function

6. **Carman, C.C. & Evans, J.** (2014). "On the Epoch of the Antikythera Mechanism and Its Eclipse Predictor." *Archive for History of Exact Sciences*, 68(6), 693-774. DOI: 10.1007/s00407-014-0145-5
   - Dating and calibration of astronomical starting point

7. **Bitsakis, Y. & Jones, A.** (2016). "The Front Dial and Parapegma Inscriptions." *Almagest*, 7(1), 68-137.
   - Analysis of front face inscriptions and calendar correlations

8. **Freeth, T., Higgon, D., Dacanalis, A., et al.** (2021). "A Model of the Cosmos in the ancient Greek Antikythera Mechanism." *Scientific Reports*, 11, 5821. DOI: 10.1038/s41598-021-84310-w
   - Proposed reconstruction of complete planetary display system

## Notes on Sources

### Reliability Assessment

- **Peer-Reviewed Scientific Papers**: Highest reliability; primary evidence
- **Museum Publications**: High reliability; direct access to artifact
- **Academic Books**: High reliability when from university presses
- **Popular Science**: Good for overview; verify claims with primary sources
- **Media/Documentaries**: Useful for introduction; always check against academic sources

### Access

Most scientific papers are available through:
- University library systems
- ResearchGate / Academia.edu (author-uploaded versions)
- Nature and journal websites (subscription or pay-per-article)
- Archaeological museum archives
